## A. New AI Applications (highest impact)

### A1. AI Onboarding Concierge
A conversational first-run agent that interviews the new member (interests, languages, city, goals), then auto-builds: first Circles, algorithm selection, feed-mode defaults, Family Tree step 1 (add parents), and a first drafted post. Converts signups into activated members — the single biggest lever on network-effect survival.
*Module: Identity/Home · Phase 1–2 · Effort: M · Impact: ★★★★★*

### A2. Creator Pre-Publish Guardian (creator-first moderation)
Before publishing, the creator copilot scans content and answers: "Will this be flagged? Why? How do I fix it?" — with suggested edits and the exact rule cited. Industry trend for 2026 is proactive, transparent creator moderation rather than strikes after the fact. Cuts moderation load and disputes simultaneously.
*Module: Creator Studio / Moderation · Phase 2–3 · Effort: M · Impact: ★★★★★*

### A3. Live Intelligence Layer
For Live rooms and events: real-time translated captions (speech → text → translate → subtitle, live), audience question clustering ("34 people asked about pricing"), a host whisper-assistant, and automatic highlight marking during the stream (not only after). Turns your existing post-live AI asset pipeline into a real-time differentiator.
*Module: Live · Phase 2 · Effort: L · Impact: ★★★★☆*

### A4. Heritage Interview Agent
A guided voice-conversation mode for elders: the AI asks gentle biographical questions in the person's own language, records, transcribes, structures answers into the Family Heritage Archive, and links them to the Person Record and timeline. Deeply aligned with your intergenerational-archive vision; no major platform offers this.
*Module: Family Tree / Heritage AI · Phase 6 · Effort: M · Impact: ★★★★★ (unique)*

### A5. Commerce & Services Copilot
For marketplace sellers and service providers: generate product listings from photos, suggest prices from comparable sales, draft service quotes, forecast demand, and power a live-shopping host assistant. Pairs naturally with your margin model.
*Module: Marketplace · Phase 4 · Effort: M · Impact: ★★★★☆*

### A6. Ad Creative Intelligence+
Extend the AI Advertising Engine with: brand-voice training (ads that sound like the business), predicted-performance scoring before spend, creative-fatigue detection ("CTR decaying — rotate variant B"), and auto-generated multilingual variants. Advertisers get agency-grade output at $0.50 CPM floors.
*Module: Advertising · Phase 5 · Effort: M · Impact: ★★★★☆*

### A7. Wellbeing & Session Intelligence
An opt-in AI that detects doom-scroll patterns and offers gentle interventions (feed-mode suggestion, break nudge, "Positive Content" algorithm), plus a personal wellbeing dashboard. Directly pre-empts the EU-style regulator scrutiny your blueprint already flags — and is marketable as a trust feature.
*Module: Feeds / Safety · Phase 2 · Effort: S · Impact: ★★★★☆*

### A8. Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Registry
Verified business/service agents that user assistants can talk to: "Ask Kaluta Travel Services agent for a Kilimanjaro trek quote." Agent identity badges, permission scopes, rate limits, paid agent slots — a new B2B revenue stream on top of your agent-readable architecture.
*Module: Developer Platform / AI Layer · Phase 7 · Effort: L · Impact: ★★★★☆ (future revenue)*

### A9. Community Early-Warning System
For community owners and moderators: sentiment drift detection, dispute-escalation prediction ("this thread is 80% likely to require intervention"), and weekly community-health summaries. Builds on the AI Community Manager.
*Module: Communities / Forums · Phase 3 · Effort: M · Impact: ★★★☆☆*

### A10. AI Memory Resurfacing
Extends the Personal Knowledge Vault: spaced, proactive resurfacing ("You saved this cassava farming proposal 6 months ago — a related thread is trending in Kigoma Forum"). Converts saved content into recurring engagement.
*Module: Knowledge Vault / Feeds · Phase 7 · Effort: S · Impact: ★★★☆☆*

*Module: Payments/Accounting · Phase 4 · Effort: L · Impact: ★★★★★*
