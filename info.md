# Kaluta Society — Blueprint Research Summary (from 16-page comprehensive specification)

## Positioning
"Kaluta Society is an AI-powered global social operating system that unifies social networking, forums, communities, multilingual communication, family relationships, digital memories, creator monetization, commerce, advertising and intelligent AI agents in one interconnected ecosystem."

It combines the strongest concepts of Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X/Threads, Reddit, Discord, LinkedIn, Discourse, Bluesky and Fediverse platforms — and goes beyond via AI agents, user-controlled algorithms, multilingual audiovisual communication, family genealogy, digital memorials, AI knowledge extraction and an integrated creator economy.

## MANDATORY BLUEPRINT REFINEMENTS (must be visibly implemented)
1. **Newsfeed conflict resolved → Multiple Feed Modes**: Following (strict reverse-chronological), For You (AI-personalized), Circles, Friends, Local, Country, Global, Topics, Trending, New. Every recommended post has "Why am I seeing this?", "Show less like this", "Change my algorithm".
2. **Algorithm Marketplace**: user-selectable algorithms — Chronological, Friends First, Family First, Local News, Business, Technology, Entertainment, Learning, Politics, Positive Content, Long-form, Video Only, Audio Only, New Creators, Global Discovery. Developers can publish feed algorithms via API.
3. **Advertising commission**: an ad purchase is Kinjy revenue in full, so the advertiser's sponsor is paid the standard 20% direct commission on the whole purchase, and 5% goes to the Kinjy Leaders pool. *(Superseded the 10% multi-level ad pool.)*
4. **Self-service account deletion**: Settings → Account → Deactivate or Delete, identity confirmation, optional cooling period, no justification required, GDPR/PDPA compliant.
5. **Passkeys instead of central biometrics**: device-level passkeys (phone/computer biometrics unlock locally); no central fingerprint/facial database. Plus email OTP, authenticator apps, hardware keys, login alerts, device management.
6. **Family Tree architecture**: genealogical relationship GRAPH as backend truth (Person Nodes + Relationship Edges: parent_of, spouse_of, adoptive_parent_of...); derived relationships (grandparent, uncle, cousin…) computed dynamically. Kaluta "Level" model (Level 0 = owner, Level 1 Parents above, Level 1 Children below, Level 2…, indefinite depth, lazy loading) as UI presentation. Confirmation workflow (PENDING→VERIFIED), 3 closely-related-member corroboration for deceased, dispute workflow, relationship closeness ranking (full siblings before half), person-centered re-rooting ("view tree from this person"), "How are we related?" path finder, common ancestor finder, duplicate detection (never auto-merge), tree merging via shared Person Nodes. AI never infers paternity/religion/ethnicity; never fabricates relatives.
7. **Family Heritage AI**: upload old photos/letters/recordings → AI family history, interactive timeline, documentary, biographies, searchable archive. Original files always preserved + AI-enhanced copies.
8. **Digital Graveyard 2.0**: memorials with biography, timeline, photos/videos/voice, guest book, condolences, digital flowers & candles (free + paid), grave location (lat/long, never fabricated, verified capture), QR memorial codes, anniversary reminders (10d/3d/6h), memorial audio with autoplay ON/OFF, up to 3 administrators with succession, pending-approval content moderation, faith styles chosen ONLY by documented wishes/admins (never AI-inferred), future AR memorials, digital legacy contacts, death verification states (UNCONFIRMED→REPORTED→UNDER REVIEW→VERIFIED).
9. **Agency pricing model**: seller $100 + Kinjy 20% markup ($20) = $120 customer price. The markup is Kinjy's revenue and the only money a commission is ever taken from; the seller's price is never touched. Applies wherever Kinjy acts as an agent between a buyer and a seller — digital products, private subscription-based clubs, services.
10. **Creator monetization**: ad revenue share — creator 40%; the direct commission and the Leaders pool are then computed on what Kinjy retains, so one advertising dollar never pays a commission twice. Plus subscriptions, tips, gifts, paid livestreams, ticketed events, paid newsletters, courses, digital products.
11. **Kinjy Leaders**: 5% of monthly company revenue, allocated to the 10,000 members with the highest **direct commissions earned during the calendar month**. A member's share = their commission that month ÷ the total commission of those top 10,000. The 5% is accrued into a liability as each revenue event is recognised, so the pool is funded money rather than a promise. Monthly snapshot → fraud review → payment batch; commission that was later reversed does not count towards a place.
12. **Subscriptions**: Free ($0, genuinely useful) / Basic $3.99/mo or $39/yr (HD video, advanced translation, scheduled posts, more AI credits, newsletters) / Premium $9.99/mo or $99/yr (4K, AI Creator Studio, dubbing, lip-sync, AI clips, brand kit, custom algorithm feeds, API allowance, premium themes). One-off purchases priced 2–4× implied subscription unit cost.
13. **Competitive ad floor pricing (auction floors, AI-adjusted)**: Standard CPM $0.50 / Premium video CPM $1.00 / CPC $0.05 / CPV $0.005 / Engagement $0.02 / Lead $0.25 min / Local promoted post from $1/day / City sponsorship from $5/day / Country & Global sponsorship by auction. AI Advertising Engine: "Create a $100 campaign promoting my restaurant to people aged 25–45 within 20 km of Dar es Salaam" → AI recommends objective, generates creatives, selects audiences, allocates budget, A/B tests; human approval before launch.
14. **Translation without barriers**: provider-independent language gateway; auto language detection, one-click translation, side-by-side view; video speech recognition → transcript → translation → subtitles → AI dubbing → voice-preserving dubbing → lip sync. Model routing by language/quality/price/latency/privacy.
15. **AI Gateway**: never hard-coded to one provider (Kimi, DeepSeek, others); router selects model by task, accuracy, language, cost, speed, data sensitivity.
16. **Cloud display mode** (signature visual identity): soft translucent panels, subtle depth, floating cards, rounded surfaces, configurable background, low visual clutter — alongside Light, Dark, System modes.
17. **Safety**: layered moderation (automated → community → platform → appeals → transparency), child-safe public browsing / teen mode / adult mode, Community Notes (AI suggests context, never auto-declares truth), content provenance labels (Original Upload / Edited / AI Assisted / AI Generated / Verified Source).
18. **AI agents across the platform**: personal AI assistant per user, Autonomous Creator Agents (recurring tasks with approval workflows), AI Community Managers, AI Forum Assistants (summaries, Q&A from forum knowledge, duplicate detection), Forum-to-Knowledge transformation (discussions → structured knowledge base with citations), Personal Knowledge Vault, agent-readable platform (semantic APIs, licensing, paid AI access).
19. **Gamification**: Helpful Contributor, Expert, Community Builder, Verified Creator, Family Historian, Local Expert badges — rewarding quality, not volume.
20. **KYC**: KinjyKYC API integration, $10 annual verification, required before earning commission or withdrawing; only verification results stored on-platform.
21. **Financial ledger**: immutable Transaction Ledger + General Ledger (Singapore accounting); wallet balances reconcile to ledger entries. Anti-fraud: self-referral, click fraud, fake KYC, bot farms detection.
22. **Direct affiliate programme (authoritative, replaces the 10-level programme)**: exactly one level. The member who sponsored the person generating a transaction is paid **20% of Kinjy's revenue** on it — and nobody above them is paid anything. There is no upline, no depth, no level-2..10 residual. Where Kinjy acts as an agent, that 20% comes out of the 20% markup (see #9), never out of the seller's price. A member with no sponsor has their commission recorded as *unclaimed* against the platform rather than silently absorbed, so the programme's take-up is reportable.
23. **Custodial escrow and dispute resolution**: buyer money for a marketplace or digital-product order is held by an **authorised financial institution** acting as custodian (Mangopay, an Electronic Money Institution, in a segregated client-money account) — not by Kinjy. It is released to the seller only once the buyer confirms receipt, or after the confirmation window (default 7 days) closes with nothing contested. Either party may open a dispute, which freezes the release clock at both ends: the seller cannot wait out a buyer raising a problem, and a silent seller is escalated to Kinjy arbitration after the response deadline. Outcomes are release to seller, refund the buyer, or a split — and on a partial refund the seller's price, Kinjy's markup and the sponsor's commission all shrink together, because a commission cannot be paid out of money that went back to the buyer. On a full refund no revenue is recognised and no commission accrues.
24. **Referral pool**: members buy a seat for $100. Sign-ups that arrive **without a personal invitation link** are assigned to a seat drawn at random, and that seat holder becomes their sponsor with exactly the same 20% commission as a personal invitation. The pool closes to new entrants at 10,000 seats. *Seats already bought keep receiving assignments after the cap is reached* — a seat that stopped working the moment the last one sold would make the final purchases worthless. Every draw is recorded (which seat, how many seats were in the draw) so an assignment can be reviewed rather than trusted.

## KALUTA ASSISTANT (mandatory embedded AI agent)
A professional AI agent packed with knowledge of ALL website functionalities and features:
- Floating agent icon at a conspicuous location that **moves dynamically depending on the user's active module** (e.g., near composer on Create, beside the memorial actions on Digital Graveyard, bottom-right on feeds).
- Users chat in **writing or voice** (speech input).
- Responses offered in **two options**: (a) written answer embedded with illustrative images, (b) a demo **video-clip** walkthrough explaining the item.
- Replies in the **same language the user used**.
- **Role-aware**: users vs admins get answers matching their access level.
- **Self-updating**: ingests codebase changelogs so its knowledge updates when features change.
- **AI industry watch**: observes AI/industry developments, notifies admin of worthwhile developments with (1) reason to adopt, (2) proposed codebase/implementation, (3) an option for admin to instruct it to execute the codebase change.
- Best practices: permission-scoped answers, labeled AI content, no fabrication.
